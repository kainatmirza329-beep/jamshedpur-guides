JAMSHEDPUR GUIDE

Complete Guide to Steel City

HEART OF CITY

1. Tata Steel Plant

Founded 1907 by Jamshedji Tata. Asia's first steel plant. Visit Tata Zoo and Marine Drive at night.

MOST FAMOUS

2. Jubilee Park

500 acre park with light show. Famous on 3rd March Founder's Day. Best for picnic.

LAKE

3. Dimna Lake

Beautiful lake at Dalma base. Boating, jetski and sunset view. 13km from city.

TREK

4. Dalma Hills

Trek to top for full city view. Home to wild elephants. Best in winter.

FOOD

5. Bistupur Market

Best market for shopping and cafes. Try Brubeck Bakery and Sakchi market.
