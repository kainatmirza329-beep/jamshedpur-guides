JAMSHEDPUR

The Steel City of India

1. Tribal Culture Centre - Jamshedpur

Tribal Culture Centre showcases rich tribal heritage of Jharkhand. Statue of Jamsetji Tata at entrance and tribal art inside.

2. Jubilee Park - Jamshedpur

Heart of Jamshedpur, 225 acres, gifted by Tata Steel in 1958. Famous for gardens and lights on 3rd March.

3. Dimna Lake - Jamshedpur

Beautiful lake at foothills of Dalma Hills. Perfect for boating, picnics and sunset view.

4. Hudco Lake - Jamshedpur

Peaceful lake in Telco. Famous for musical fountain and evening walk track.

5. Subarnarekha Riverfront - Jamshedpur

Misty morning view over Subarnarekha River is very peaceful and scenic for nature lovers.

6. Tata Steel Plant - Jamshedpur

Identity of Jamshedpur. Asia's first steel company in 1907. Reason Jamshedpur is Steel City.

7. Keenan Stadium - Jamshedpur

Historic cricket stadium in Bistupur. Hosted many international matches.

8. Jubilee Amusement Park - Jamshedpur

Inside Jubilee Park. Full of fun for kids - toy train, rides and cartoon statues.

About Jamshedpur - 10 Lines

1. Jamshedpur is famously known as the Steel City of India.
2. It was envisioned by Jamsetji Tata and founded in 1907.
3. It is located in Jharkhand at meeting point of Subarnarekha and Kharkai rivers.
4. It is India's first planned industrial city.
5. Tata Steel, Asia's first steel company, is situated here.
6. The city is known for greenery, cleanliness and best infrastructure.
7. Famous places like Jubilee Park, Dimna Lake, Hudco Lake attract tourists.
8. Tribal Culture Centre preserves tribal culture of Jharkhand.
9. Keenan Stadium and JRD Tata Sports Complex make it a sports city also.
10. Jamshedpur is perfect example where industry, nature and culture live together.
