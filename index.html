JAMSHEDPUR

The Steel City of India - Jharkhand

1. Tribal Culture Centre

JAMSHEDPUR

The Tribal Culture Centre is the cultural soul of Jamshedpur. Located near Jubilee Park, it was established to preserve and promote the rich tribal heritage of Jharkhand. The centre displays tribal art, artifacts, lifestyle and traditions of Ho, Santhal and Munda communities. The iconic statue of Jamsetji Tata at the entrance welcomes visitors.

2. Jubilee Park

JAMSHEDPUR

Jubilee Park is known as the heart of Jamshedpur. Spread over 225 acres, this park was gifted by Tata Steel in 1958 and was inaugurated by Jawaharlal Nehru. Inspired by Vrindavan Gardens of Mysore, it is famous for its illuminated gardens, fountains and laser show during Tata's Founder's Day on 3rd March.

3. Dimna Lake

JAMSHEDPUR

Dimna Lake is a stunning artificial reservoir nestled at the foot of Dalma Hills, just 13 km from the city. Surrounded by lush green forests and mountains, the lake is famous for boating, jet skiing and breathtaking sunset views. It is a favorite picnic spot for families and nature lovers.

4. Hudco Lake

JAMSHEDPUR

Hudco Lake is a serene lake located in the Telco Colony area of Jamshedpur. Known for its peaceful environment, musical fountain and well-maintained walking track, it is perfect for morning walks and evening relaxation. The lake reflects the calm and green lifestyle of Jamshedpur.

5. Subarnarekha Riverfront

JAMSHEDPUR

Subarnarekha River is one of the lifelines of Jamshedpur. Flowing through the city, its misty morning view with golden sunrise is truly magical. The riverfront is a peaceful place for photography, meditation and enjoying nature's silence away from city noise.

6. Tata Steel Plant

JAMSHEDPUR

Tata Steel Plant is the identity and pride of Jamshedpur. Established in 1907, it is Asia's first steel company and the reason why Jamshedpur is called the Steel City of India. This plant not only built the city but also gave India its first planned industrial city model.

7. Keenan Stadium

JAMSHEDPUR

Keenan Stadium is a historic international cricket stadium in the Bistupur area. Named after John Lawrence Keenan, a former General Manager of Tata Steel, this stadium has hosted many Ranji Trophy and international matches. It is a symbol of Jamshedpur's sports culture.

8. Jubilee Amusement Park

JAMSHEDPUR

Located inside Jubilee Park, the Jubilee Amusement Park is a fun-filled destination for kids and families. With its colorful cartoon characters, toy train, giant wheel and joy rides, it brings joy to every visitor. The park is beautifully maintained by Tata Steel.
