JAMSHEDPUR

The Steel City of India

1. Tribal Culture Centre

Tribal heritage of Jharkhand, statue of Jamsetji Tata at entrance.

2. Jubilee Park

Heart of city, 225 acres, gifted by Tata Steel, famous for lights on Founder's Day.

3. Dimna Lake

Artificial lake at Dalma Hills, perfect for boating and sunset view.

4. Hudco Lake

Peaceful lake in Telco, famous for musical fountain and walk track.

5. Subarnarekha Riverfront

Misty morning view, very peaceful for nature lovers.

6. Tata Steel Plant

Asia's first steel company 1907, identity of Steel City.

7. Keenan Stadium

Historic cricket stadium, hosted international matches.

8. Jubilee Amusement Park

Fun for kids - toy train, rides and cartoon statues.
